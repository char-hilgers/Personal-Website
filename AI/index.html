    Values

    I value integrity, in the sense of wholeness. Work and personal life are not separable. I honour the breadth of my relations: to people and other beings, animals, minerals, to the earth. As a scientist, I explore research questions which have meaning imbued in them by their implications to myself and my relations. I attend to the reality of suffering that can result from my actions and seek to repair where I have harmed.

    Statement on Large Language Models, Machine Learning, and "Artificial Intelligence"

    Last updated 2026-11-26

    LLMs

    Large language models (LLMs) can produce flashy results, but the bigger social reorganization occurring now is around the infrastructure that feeds LLMs (Nicole Nelson, panel discussion). LLM data centres and massive electricity requirements are introducing major ecological devastation in an era of ongoing and accelerating climate crisis. This is unwise and not an inevitability. Furthermore, profit-driven businesses are pushing use of these tools.

    I currently do not use LLMs for research hypothesis generation or for solving research problems. I am open to using local LLMs and a workplace-provided LLM that is run on Microsoft Azure cloud infrastructure, in Europe, and which can be severed from the internet and accessed only via Intranet. The latter I might use for literature review, translating from one coding language to another (eg. R to Python), coding support (like that found on Stack Overflow). For my own code and writing and that of my collaborators, I will not put their or our unpublished original work into an LLM connected to the internet.

    Translation

    I occasionally use neural network translator DeepL for first-pass English-German translation. I always read and edit the translation afterwards, and if I do not understand something, I look it up in a dictionary. At the moment I am not letting others know when I've done this. This personal AI statement serves to communicate its use. I will include a statement like "Translated with support from DeepL" when I have used it for publications.

    This is the full extent of my LLM and neural network use.

    Collaboration

    Beginning a project together, we can put together an agreement about our use of LLMs and other "artificial intelligence" tools.

    The agreement would ideally cover: idea generation, literature search, summarizing/reviewing literature, code, documentation, first drafts of articles, later drafts of articles, use of sensitive data, how we communicate with each other (via emails or chat) and what we disclose about this, a plan to regularly revisit and update the agreement, attending to the material and ecological impact of our intended uses, and deciding which LLMs under which conditions (local, possibility of sandboxing, etc.). This agreement would be made with consideration of collaborators' legal and workplace requirements, and with our ethics and values.

    Expectations

    I am uneasy with being an editor or corrector for content produced by an LLM, which can be generated in large volumes very quickly. As use of LLMs proliferates in science, we are at risk of producing more but understanding less (Messeri and Crockett 2024).

    Any time someone produces a document with an LLM and sends it to me to comment or work on further, if they have not checked and changed it substantially themselves, I will open a conversation with the sender about the nature of our work relationship. I prefer to be asked to do a specific task rather than be sent LLM output and asked to improve or correct it.
